# キャラクター画像生成のプロンプト記録

## 2026-10-16 採用の更新

この文書の下段は制作履歴。最終採用は、その後にチャットで生成・承認された日本の漫画風の目・表情の五図シート。最新の[美術方針](CHARACTER_ART_DIRECTION.md)を優先する。再制作ではそのシートを参照画像として、衣装・種族・比率を固定し、目を細くするのではなく、開いた漫画の目・線の強弱・豊かな驚きや喜びを維持する。陰影や素材の立体感は残せる。

追加ポーズと背景の制作指示は[紙芝居ムービー設計](PAPER_THEATER_STORYBOARDS.md)に記載。今回は背景や追加ポーズの画像生成はまだ行っていない。

## 採用済みC3：通常3ポーズ・ちび2ポーズ

ユーザー指定により全員C3を採用。参照は白背景のアニメ調比較シートの右端C3。各人の上段N1/N2/N3・下段S1/S2を制作し、合計15図をチャットに表示した。動画・透過パーツ・3Dモデルではない。

### NAGI：最終ポーズ制作

Use case: identity-preserve / stylized-concept. Create a character acting and chibi model sheet using the supplied image as the identity reference. THE USER SELECTED THE RIGHTMOST C3 DESIGN ONLY. Every normal-sized figure must be exactly that same C3 character: preserve its face construction, eyes, muzzle/beak, markings, fur/feather colors, body proportions, clothing cut, accessories and approved clean anime drawing style. Do not blend in C1 or C2 and do not redesign the character. New work consists only of poses and facial expressions plus a consistent chibi adaptation. PURE WHITE #FFFFFF background, neutral clean daylight colors, no yellow/sepia/ivory cast, no paper grain or aged texture. Intentional anime contours and clean cel-style shadows consistent with C3. One generous landscape sheet with FIVE complete separate full-body drawings, no cropping or overlap: top row THREE full-sized normal-proportion poses at equal scale labelled N1, N2, N3; bottom row TWO chibi versions centred and clearly smaller, labelled S1, S2. Chibi versions have approximately two-heads-tall proportions but retain the same species, face markings, costume colors and signature accessories. Only a small romanized character name as heading and those five short labels; no other text, no scenery, no extra characters, no speech balloons, no UI. Leave useful blank margins. The normal poses must be genuinely different silhouettes and expressions, suitable as future arcade event illustration assets. These are full character poses, not just head studies. NAGI C3 is the slim youthful upright river otter courier: brown fur, light neutral cream muzzle/chest, small round ears, long thick tapered tail, cropped open green jacket with rolled sleeves and white inner shirt, diagonal brown leather postal satchel with brass buckle. N1 NORMAL: planted friendly upright stance, one paw gently waving and the other on the mailbag strap, relaxed open smile. N2 ANTICIPATION: leaning forward and slightly crouched, both paws holding a folded route map at chest height, brows raised, eyes focused wide, mouth a small tense line as if watching the ball approach a hole; tail lifted for balance. N3 JOY: a buoyant celebratory hop with one paw lifted high holding a plain envelope, other paw out, beaming smile with eyes joyfully curved, tail following the jump. S1 CHIBI NORMAL: two-head-tall otter, small green jacket, satchel, prominent otter tail, cheerful little wave. S2 CHIBI JOY: same chibi identity in a compact happy jump, both paws lifted and a wide happy smile, satchel and tail still legible. No human body or human hair.

## 採用前の比較の記録


2026-10-15。組み込みの画像生成機能を使用。白背景のアニメ調比較から、全員C3を採用した。以下は採用前比較の実際のプロンプト。参照画像は初回A/B/Cシートの右端C。生成画像はこの相談のチャットに表示している。

## NAGI：C1/C2/C3比較

Use case: stylized-concept. Edit the supplied character comparison sheet into a NEW wide three-column character design exploration sheet. IMPORTANT REFERENCE: use ONLY the RIGHTMOST character, labelled C, as the identity and silhouette base for ALL THREE new concepts; the old left and middle variants are not the requested reference. Keep the character's species, essential clothing, occupation, signature accessories and recognizable face markings from that rightmost C. Reinterpret this base into three visibly different Japanese anime-style character designs, still the same person. Exactly three full-body characters at the same scale, with clean spacing and no cropping. Background must be PURE WHITE #FFFFFF, completely clean, no cream, no ivory, no yellow color cast, no aged paper, no paper grain, no beige wash, no sepia, no vintage grading. Neutral daylight color balance. Bright clean local colors, deep forest green clothes, clear white highlights, charcoal shadows; natural fur/feather colors without applying a warm global filter. Use confident economical anime linework, deliberate shape design, crisp two- or three-tone cel shading. These must look like polished professional Japanese animation character concepts, not painterly western storybook art, not a plush toy and not a photoreal animal. No specific studio, artist, franchise or existing character imitation. Retain animal anatomy rather than turning the animal into a human with ears. Three columns are labelled only C1, C2, C3, with a small romanized character name at the top. C1 = approachable television anime design with clear rounded expressive features and a clean silhouette. C2 = dynamic arcade anime design with somewhat sharper expressions, stronger graphic shapes and bold clean contour, but not aggressive. C3 = refined animated-feature anime design with more elegant proportions and finer restrained linework, still clearly cel-shaded rather than watercolor. Differences should include face construction, silhouette and costume simplification, not merely poses or color swaps. No additional characters, props unrelated to the role, logos, interface mockups or scenery. Character NAGI, rightmost reference C: a slim upright river otter courier with an alert youthful expression, small round ears, thick long tapered tail, dark brown fur, light neutral cream-white muzzle and chest. A cropped open deep-green jacket with rolled sleeves and a clear white inner shirt, cross-body brown leather mail satchel with brass buckle, nautical folded route sheet. Keep that active adventurous courier identity and long otter tail. Slightly vary the silhouette and face in each anime interpretation; cheerful and capable, not babyish. Natural dark brown fur may remain brown but the entire sheet must stay clean and neutral, not amber.
